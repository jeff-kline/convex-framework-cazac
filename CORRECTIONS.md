## Correction, withdrawal, and supersession policy

- A typographical correction that cannot change a mathematical conclusion may
  be made on the living branch and recorded here when readers could otherwise
  be misled.
- A material correction creates a new semantic version, tag, and permanent
  archive. The earlier archive remains public and is linked from this history.
- A false principal claim is marked withdrawn in the living README, this file,
  and the repository release history. The original version remains findable
  with a warning and an explanation.
- A later result that replaces this work is recorded as a supersession, not as
  a silent rewrite of the earlier archive.
- Suspected errors may be reported through the public repository issue tracker.
